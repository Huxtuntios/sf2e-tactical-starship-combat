## Reliability follow-up — unpublished, source/mock tested only

This section supersedes conflicting historical notes below for the changed paths. The module version and manifest compatibility declarations are unchanged. No live Foundry, actual Toolbelt execution, multi-client privacy or GM failover was exercised.

- Sensor Map retains its background node and one asynchronous source/revision loader across part replacement. Giant rasters use a PNG display copy capped at 4096 pixels, with original path/file unchanged; ordinary images are not rescaled. Initial full-image decoding still consumes memory, and tainted/CORS-restricted canvases or unavailable encoding can fall back to the full native image. Failed/timed-out loads need an explicit source/revision refresh rather than automatic repeated retries. Same-URL content replacement needs `invalidateSensorMapBackground()` or a Combat update with `tscForceMapBackground: true`. Actor caches are window-owned and pruned to visible known UUIDs; actual Foundry synthetic/compendium hook delivery remains unverified.
- Socket waits are bounded: 15 seconds initially, then one 120-second result deadline after the first eligible acknowledgement. Local GM waits are bounded too. Disconnect/active-GM changes reject outstanding requests; delayed replies cannot revive them. A timeout only ends the caller's wait: the handler may still finish and write documents. Inspect state and chat before retrying. These queues and bounded receipts do **not** guarantee distributed exactly-once processing.
- Toolbelt manual ×2/×3 is not critical-hit evidence. The original selected roll's degree or an unambiguous native single-roll attack damage outcome is used; associated save/check or malformed contexts cannot prove a critical hit. Without context, a saved degree relies on the provider's original metadata, not independent attack verification. Missing/contradictory metadata stays noncritical. The starship's own critical save failure also warrants enhanced Compromised: existing target-bound save paths remain unchanged, but Toolbelt's manual ×2 cannot establish that target's result. Use the explicit GM damage path for such ambiguous applications rather than infer it from scaling. Scaling, typed IWR/Hardness/shields and damage receipts are unchanged. Source review of PF2e 8.6.1/SF2e 1.6.1 and Toolbelt 3.60.0 is not live integration verification; newer/modified providers remain untested.
- Undo persists a private intent before restoring the Actor and writes one bounded Actor receipt with the restore. Retry can finish history finalization only when receipt, restored snapshot and Combat guard still match; Actor-before alone is not proof. Object-key order is ignored, array order and missing/null/zero remain significant. An unfinished receipt blocks another Undo on that Actor, and an oldest pending intent blocks history appends rather than being evicted beyond 80 records. Inspect and reconcile conflicts manually; do not delete recovery evidence blindly. HP/SP, damage and critical-state Undo remain prohibited.
- Reveal recovery finishes forward from a private journal intent; it cannot undo information already disclosed. Stages are advisory: actual map/Actor/Combatant identity, ownership, initiative and duplicates are rechecked. Conflicts keep the contact pending for GM inspection; completed journal receipts retain only the latest 64 reveals. Local serialization is not a distributed lock, and external document edits can still conflict. The GM map button exposes pending recovery even after public placement; public edits/initiative for its key/Actor and resizing are guarded, while independent public marker additions do not require a valid private journal. No render/hook initiates recovery writes.
- The ordered, unexecuted live-world checklist is in `docs/testing.md`. A real server with separate GM/player clients is required before claiming compatibility or confidentiality. No action or reaction accounting, world-wide migration, release publication or system upgrade is added.

Harry's conditions remain module-only and do not become native conditions through Toolbelt.

## Gameplay and integration boundaries

- Native SF2e damage applied directly to a ship does not use the module's Shield Point handling. Use the module's own damage tools.
- Module-managed effects support turn/round timing with stable combatant anchors. Arbitrary conditions and sustaining require explicit confirmation; the module does not infer whether narrative conditions happened. Removing an anchored combatant or changing an encounter manually warrants a GM review of pending effects.
- Undo is limited to writes recorded by the module, and is refused when the affected state has changed incompatibly. It is not a general Foundry/world rollback.
- Station and upgrade rules can expose executable actions, contextual checks, damage and condition links, but this is not full rules automation. Unrecognized prose, source references and nonstandard effects remain GM adjudications. Placeholder prices require GM review. Drones and expansion bays remain limited.
- Multiple attack penalty is tracked for supported module actions and system rolls with readable flags. Mixed workflows, custom rolls and shared-crew economies may require manual correction.
- Hazard routines automate supported damage/check patterns; other effects are posted for GM resolution. Family abilities and book references may remain descriptive.
- Station markers and automatic helming on the scene map assume square grids.
- The Sensor Map is a zone-based module window, not a replacement for every Foundry canvas feature. Image paths must be accessible to players; arbitrary remote hosts may block loading.
- Detection and player visibility depend on GM-authoritative encounter state. Test them with a separate player account, not only the GM preview.
- UI themes and window preferences are client-side; encounter artwork, background and custom map colors are shared. Custom colors may need adjustment for contrasting client themes. Native select popup rendering varies by browser and operating system.
- Conditions and typed damage rely on supported SF2e APIs and enrichers. Validate behavior after system updates.
- Modules detected for coexistence are not bundled or required, and compatibility with arbitrary module combinations is not guaranteed.
